# Redesign and Research

## Current Issues

The Craigslist UI has usability issues, most of which stem from the UI's step away from an industry standard template for a webpage
and aesthetic design. To elaborate, the color scheme, indentation, and logical grouping of elements is unintuitive and not learnable,
as the existing knowledge users are likely to have on navigating web pages is not invoked.

These issues, especially that of learnability,
are serious, as there are countless alternatives users could opt for instead of Craigslist, not to mention AI tools, which can parse
the information available on Craigslist and deliver it to them in a highly readable way.

While the general goals of the UI would remain the same, our redesign will benefit the UI by putting an emphasis on navigation and comparison.
We will allow users to navigate the site more efficiently, allowing them to find the services they need with ease. After they find the service they need, we will then allow them to efficiently compare between the available options and conduct business.
