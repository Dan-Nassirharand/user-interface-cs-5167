## Task 4: Survey

### Method

A short survey (Canvas method h) was used to get opinions about the Craigslist Cincinnati homepage.

The questions asked about Craigslist experience, finding a used desk, problems with the homepage, and ideas for improvement.

Two roommates answered the questions. One classmate has not answered yet. The responses below summarize their answers.

### Findings

#### 1. Finding the category was difficult

Both participants thought a used desk would be in the furniture category. However, they did not know where to find that category on the homepage.

The category name was understandable, but its location was not clear.

#### 2. There was too much text

Both participants said the amount of text made the page difficult to use. It was hard to find the category they wanted among many links.

#### 3. The categories should be kept

Both participants wanted to keep the categories. They suggested reducing the amount of text instead of removing the category structure.

### Design Ideas

- Make category titles larger and add more space between groups.
- Show main categories first and let users open more links when needed.
- Keep familiar category names, such as furniture.

These ideas need to be tested in the prototype to check whether they make categories easier to find.

### Limitations

Only two roommates answered the survey, so the results do not represent all Craigslist users.

The survey collected opinions. It did not measure how long participants took or how many errors they made.
