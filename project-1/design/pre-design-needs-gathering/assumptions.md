# Assumptions

- All LED features produce an aesthetically pleasing result
- Price of the shoe is comparable to what is currently available in industry.
- The electrical components involved in the manufacture of the shoe are resilient.
  - i.e. scratch resistant touchscreens, LEDs that don't burn out/break, etc.
- The overall lifetime of the shoe should be comparable to what is currently available in industry.
- Shoe can both store and output enough energy in such a way that a user experiences a
  noticeable increase in performance and comfort in their movement.
- Electrical components can withstand adverse weather conditions (moderate/severe heat and cold).
- The weight of the electrical components of the shoe will not hinder user movement and be comparable
  to weight of existing shoes available for purchase today.
- Electrical components pose no higher risk to users than comparable embedded devices.
  - ex. heat/shock risk associated with electrically-warmed gloves and hats.
- The presence of electrical components, especially the button placed on the inside of the shoe, will not
  hinder user comfort or movement.
