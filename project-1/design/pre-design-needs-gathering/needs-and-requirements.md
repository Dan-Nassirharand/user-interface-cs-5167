# User Needs and Design Requirements

This collection of user needs and design requirements will serve as the user-centered
foundation of the project. The designer will be led primarily by these principles gathered
during [user interviews](./interviews.md) to design a product to best serve their needs.

UN-1: User needs to be able to purchase shoes at a reasonable cost
DR-1: Shoes shall be available for purchase at a competitive price.

UN-2: User needs to easily take shoes on/off
DR-2: Shoes shall have a hands-free method for the user to wear/take off without sacrificing walking utility

UN-3: User needs to be able to walk comfortably in shoe
DR-3: Shoes shall enhance the comfort and efficiency of walking compared to competitor shoes

UN-4: Users need to satisfy their personal aesthetic taste in purchasing shoes
DR-4: Shoes shall provide a way for users to realize their personal aesthetic tastes

UN-5: Users have various utility needs they expect their shoes to satisfy
DR-5: Shoes shall allow users to adjust the utility performance of the shoe
